Product platform

Level 2 Chargers

Charging-hardware development for commercial electric-mobility applications. Product specifications will be published as designs and supporting documentation are finalized.

Portable Chargers

Portable charging concepts centered on practical connection, mobility and application needs, with final capabilities communicated as individual products mature.

Charging Cables

Charging cable development, build and verification with attention to conductors, insulation, shielding, jacket, termination, connector construction, durability and connection integrity.

Extension Systems

Extension hardware considered as part of the broader cable and connection platform, with application suitability addressed at the project level.

Control Boards

Electronic control capability supporting connected charging hardware and future application-specific product architectures.

RFID Control

Access technology for use cases where identification and managed charging access are relevant.
