Connector & Interface

The connector creates the physical interface between charging equipment and the vehicle. Development considers the intended interface, handling and integration with the overall cable assembly.

Cable Construction

The cable structure brings together the electrical path and protective layers required by the intended application. Detailed materials, dimensions and proprietary construction remain product-development information.

Strain Relief & Termination

The transition between cable and connector is treated as part of the complete mechanical and electrical assembly, with handling and connection requirements considered together.

Extension & Specialized Solutions

Application-led cable development for requirements involving additional reach or specialized configurations, evaluated against the electrical, mechanical and safety needs of the intended use.
