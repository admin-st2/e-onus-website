Development approach

Move from an idea toward evidence.

The goal is not complexity for its own sake. E-Onus focuses on understanding the application, defining the hardware direction, building what can be evaluated and using results to guide the next decision.

Cable engineering

The cable is a system.

Conductors, insulation, shielding, outer jacket, termination and connector construction interact. E-Onus evaluates those relationships as part of cable development while keeping project-specific designs and specifications confidential.

Controls

Physical hardware meets electronic control.

Control-board and RFID work supports product concepts where electronic control, access and identification are relevant to the charging experience.
