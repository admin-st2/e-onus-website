Africa

Electrifying public fleets.
Hardware built around the mission.

E-Onus is developing an Africa-focused pathway centered on government entities, public-sector fleets and institutional vehicle programs. The goal is to help organizations evaluate practical charging hardware and fleet electrification requirements around how vehicles are actually operated.

Regional focus

Hardware programs shaped around the market and application.

E-Onus approaches regional opportunities through customer requirements, practical use cases and project-specific engineering. Availability, certification and regulatory requirements are evaluated as individual programs develop.

Government

Government entities

Engage ministries, agencies, municipalities and other public institutions around defined electric-vehicle fleet and charging-hardware requirements.

Public fleets

Fleet electrification

Develop charging-hardware pathways around fleet size, vehicle duty cycles, operating locations, charging windows and organizational needs.

Infrastructure

Practical deployment planning

Evaluate charging equipment, cable systems and controls in the context of the intended fleet environment and available infrastructure.

Fleet-first approach

Start with vehicles, routes, parking patterns, operating schedules and charging windows so hardware requirements follow the mission rather than a generic deployment model.

Government procurement

Support early technical scoping for public-sector procurement conversations, tenders and fleet programs while keeping formal requirements specific to the purchasing entity and jurisdiction.

Hardware platform

Level 2 charging, portable charging, cable systems, control boards and RFID access can be considered according to the needs of the fleet program.

Local conditions

Power availability, site conditions, climate, serviceability and maintainability should be considered when defining hardware for a specific country or operating environment.

Country-specific requirements

Africa is not treated as a single regulatory market. Certification, electrical, procurement and other requirements must be evaluated for the country and project involved.

Long-term usefulness

Durability, serviceability and responsible product choices are important considerations for fleet hardware intended to support long operating lives.
