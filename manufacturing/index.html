03 / QUALITY & VERIFICATION

Test what matters.
Improve what we learn.

E-Onus is developing and testing its charging cable systems. Verification should be appropriate to the design, application and stage of development—not treated as a marketing claim.

Construction review

Examine assembly consistency, physical interfaces and workmanship.

Electrical & connection checks

Define suitable checks for continuity, connections and other applicable design requirements.

Documented refinement

Use test observations to guide revisions and future verification plans.

Specific certifications, test results and production capabilities are confirmed only when supported by project documentation.
